# Photo sources and licenses

Every photograph in the prototype, where it came from and under which license. This file mirrors `src/seed/photoData.ts` (generated by `scripts/photos.py`); the same credits are shown to visitors on the **Photo credits** page (`/app/credits`), which every page links to from its footer.

## Hero slideshow

The Home hero shows ten photos (`HERO_SLIDES` in `src/features/customer/Home.tsx`) without a caption or credit on the photo itself. All ten are from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licenses (CC BY or CC BY-SA), which allow reuse with attribution. Attribution is given on the Photo credits page, linked from the footer of every page. None of the hero photos is one of the team-supplied photos below.

## Photos that still need a license check

Four photos (Bolinao, Dingalan, Minalungao, Tanay) were supplied by the TAMS AP team without a stated source or license. They are used only on destination cards and pages, never in the hero. Confirm their license, or replace them, before any public use.
